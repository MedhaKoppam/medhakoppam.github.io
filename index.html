Bio

I am an MSCS student at Georgia Tech, specializing in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), with a background in software engineering and AI research. I am interested in understanding how people interact with complex technologies and designing systems that make those interactions more intuitive, effective, and human-centered.

My current work spans human-centered AI, UX research, and interactive systems. At the Play and Learn Lab at Georgia Tech, I support research on how high-school students experience and learn from AI-based educational games. Previously, as a Research Fellow at Microsoft Research India, I worked on a platform for large-scale, repository-level code changes, exploring how complex developer workflows can be made more accessible through natural-language interaction and thoughtful interface design.

My interests lie at the intersection of HCI, AI, developer tools, and learning. I enjoy combining technical implementation with user research to understand a problem from both sides: how a system works and how people actually experience it.
